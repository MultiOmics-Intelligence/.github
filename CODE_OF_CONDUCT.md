# Code of conduct

LSMA and MultiOmics Intelligence repositories are places to learn. We expect everyone to:

- Be kind and patient. Everyone here was a beginner once.
- Ask and answer questions about the work, not about the person.
- Never share personal data, patient data, passwords or keys, in code, issues or discussions.
- Do your own project work. You may discuss ideas; do not copy another student's solution or post full answers publicly.
- Give credit when you use someone else's code or idea.

Harassment, discrimination or abuse of any kind is not accepted. We may remove content and block accounts that break these rules.

To report a problem, email **info@lsma.co.uk**. Reports are read by the LSMA team and kept confidential.

Dr. Anas Alzaeem, Founder & CEO, MultiOmics Intelligence
