# Suggesting improvements

Found a mistake in a project brief, a broken link or a check that seems wrong? Thank you.

- **Students:** email info@lsma.co.uk with the repository name and what you noticed, or open an issue on the template repository using the **Improvement** form. Do not include your solution.
- Keep reports short: what you expected, what happened, and which task.

The LSMA team reviews every suggestion.
